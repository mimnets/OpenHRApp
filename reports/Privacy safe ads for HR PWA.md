# Replace the Smartlink with Affiliates and Sponsors

OpenHRApp should earn money in three ways, in this order. First, direct affiliate links to vetted business products, led by Zoho, then Deel/Remote, Fiverr and Namecheap. Second, a self-sold "Sponsor OpenHR" slot priced in BDT for local B2B advertisers. Third, a house ad for the paid ad-free plan whenever the slot is unsold. All three run through the same self-hosted image and first-party `/go/:adId` redirect, which never loads third-party code inside the PWA. The Adsterra Smartlink now in production passes the privacy test, because a plain outbound link loads no script. It fails the trust test badly. Smartlink destinations are picked by a traffic-distribution system that Adsterra and its peer Monetag have both let malware campaigns use. Bangladeshi traffic earns a fraction of a cent per click. Adsterra also has no confirmed Payoneer payout, so a Dhaka operator without PayPal faces a $100 crypto minimum or a $1,000 wire. The smartlink should come out of the signed-in app. No privacy-safe display network (EthicalAds, Carbon) will accept OpenHRApp at its current traffic, and the ones that will accept it serve crypto-themed ads that clash with an HR brand. At low traffic, revenue depends on a few high-value conversions and direct deals, not impressions. Plan on a few one-off commissions and the first one to three sponsors in year one, not a steady CPM income.

## The production smartlink is privacy-clean but trust-toxic

The current setup has one real strength. An `<a href>` to a smartlink, wrapped around a self-hosted image, loads nothing from Adsterra until the user clicks. No script can read selfies, GPS coordinates or salary data from the DOM. A plain outbound link is also much less likely to get the app's own domain flagged by Safe Browsing than on-page pop or script tags. Google's 2016 policy targets deceptive ad content *hosted on* a site, which a plain link is not ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2016/04/no-more-deceptive-download-buttons)). The research found no public case of a linking domain flagged solely for an outbound smartlink. That is absence of evidence, not proof.

The problem is where the click goes. A smartlink sends each visitor through a redirect chain to one of "thousands of different landing pages and offers" chosen by geo and device ([Monetag blog](https://monetag.com/blog/how-to-make-money-with-one-link-traffic-monetization-with-direct-links/)). The operator does not control those destinations. In the **DeceptionAds campaign of December 2024**, Monetag's TDS was the main channel delivering fake-captcha infostealers, explicitly including "direct links and smartlinks bypassing websites entirely". It reached about **1M impressions a day across 3,000+ publisher sites** and resumed after Monetag removed 200+ accounts ([Infostealers.com / Guardio](https://www.infostealers.com/article/deceptionads-fake-captcha-driving-infostealer-infections-and-a-glimpse-to-the-dark-side-of-internet-advertising/)). Adsterra has its own record. In the Master134 operation, Check Point said Adsterra was "powering the whole process" of routing hijacked traffic to exploit kits ([Check Point Research](https://research.checkpoint.com/2018/malvertising-campaign-based-secrets-lies/); [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/cybersecurity/feature/Inside-Master134-Propeller-Ads-connected-to-malvertising-campaign)). Its Trustpilot rating is suspended for guideline breaches, and October 2026 reviews complain of a site "redirecting people to malware sites" and of accounts banned just before payout ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/adsterra.com)). Adsterra's only content control is a mainstream/non-mainstream toggle, and it recommends accepting all ads, iGaming included, "to boost CPM" ([Adsterra](https://adsterra.com/blog/what-is-a-smartlink/)). If an employee clicks a "Sponsored" tile inside their employer's HR system and lands on a fake captcha or a casino, they will blame OpenHRApp, and the customer's IT team can reasonably ask for the product to be blocked.

The money does not justify that risk. No official per-click rate exists for Bangladesh. Third-party estimates put Tier-3 geos such as Bangladesh at **$0.20-0.30 CPM**, and direct links earn roughly 30-60% of pop-ad revenue ([froggyads](https://froggyads.com/monetag-cpm-rates/); [Monetag blog](https://monetag.com/blog/how-to-make-money-with-one-link-traffic-monetization-with-direct-links/)). That works out to well under a dollar per thousand clicks. Getting paid is just as hard. Sources list WebMoney/Paxum ($5), crypto ($100) and wire ($1,000), and **Payoneer is not among Adsterra's payout methods** ([thewearify](https://thewearify.com/adsterra-review/)). Adsterra also says the link "should not be placed too close to navigation buttons" ([Affpaying](https://www.affpaying.com/blog/instant-income-with-or-without-a-website-2025-adsterra-direct-link-monetization)), which is hard to guarantee in a compact PWA sidebar. The honest verdict: the smartlink was a reasonable privacy-first stopgap, but it is the wrong product for a B2B HR app. It should be removed from logged-in screens now, not later. If the owner wants to keep it briefly while affiliate approvals come through, set it to mainstream-only, keep it off every employee-facing screen, and restrict it to admin dashboards. HilltopAds and Clickadu are no improvement, because they run the same TDS model with the same risk profile.

## Affiliate links pay hundreds per conversion and need no script

Every affiliate program the research found tracks conversions on the merchant's own site after a click on a plain referral link, with no affiliate-side script. Deel attributes through a unique PartnerStack link ([Deel](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/)), Zoho tracks for 90 days from the first click ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/business.html)), and Hostinger sets a 30-day cookie on its own domain ([Hostinger FAQ](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs)). This matches the self-hosted pattern exactly. Download the approved banner from the affiliate dashboard, host it yourself, and link it through `/go`. Network-hosted image tags from Awin, CJ or Impact load an impression pixel from the network, so self-hosting the image also removes that request.

| Program | Payout | Payout route for Dhaka, no PayPal | Fit and gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zoho** (People, Books, Workplace) | 15% of 12 months' revenue, 90-day tracking, $100 min | Wire transfer, in-house | Best product fit and regional presence; register in the India data centre |
| **Deel** | $500 SQL + $1,000 customer = $1,500 | PartnerStack: Airwallex direct deposit (BD unconfirmed); Stripe unavailable | Highest upside; verify payout before integrating |
| **Remote** | 10-15% for 12 months, 90-day cookie | PartnerStack or Awin (Payoneer) | EOR fit for SMBs hiring abroad |
| **Fiverr** | 25% CPA + 10% for 12 months, $100 min | Payoneer under $1,000, wire above | Easy approval; familiar to Bangladeshi users |
| **Namecheap** (via CJ) | Per sale | Payoneer, $50 min | Domains and email for SMBs |
| Hostinger | Up to 40% | $500 bank minimum | Needs 1,000+ traffic, "focus regions"; hard to qualify |

Sources: [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/business.html), [Deel](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/), [PartnerStack payouts](https://partnerstack.com/payouts), [Remote](https://remote.com/en-us/partners/affiliates), [Awin Remote](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/75664), [strackr Fiverr](https://strackr.com/blog/fiverr-affiliate-program), [Namecheap payouts](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/9994/2241/payment-options-and-minimum-payout-limits-for-affiliates), [Hostinger FAQ](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs).

Two facts favour affiliates over any CPM product. First, one **$1,500 Deel conversion** equals millions of Bangladeshi smartlink clicks. Second, the strongest programs pay by wire or through **Payoneer, which Awin uses for all international payouts** ([Awin](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/International-Payment-Method-FAQs)) and which withdraws straight to bKash. The weak spot is PartnerStack. Bangladesh's place on its Airwallex list was reported but not confirmed (the help page returned 403), so check the PartnerStack dashboard before building Deel or Rippling slots. Skip US-only payroll tools such as Gusto and QuickBooks Payroll, which will rarely convert from a South Asian user base. Also skip the Google Workspace referral program, whose regional eligibility list did not include Bangladesh ([Google](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en/landing/partners/referral/faqs/)). Wise's $75 refer-a-friend payout is also weak, because Bangladesh residents cannot open a Wise account to receive money ([nsave](https://www.nsave.com/bangladesh/wise)).

On placement terms, none of the official pages read explicitly bans or allows a static banner inside a logged-in web app. The explicit bans found cover brand-keyword PPC (Hostinger), self-referral and reuse of brand material (Zoho), and the adware and toolbar software that industry codes target ([IAB UK](https://iabuk.com/standards-guidelines/software-application-code-conduct)). An owned in-product banner is closer to a newsletter placement, which Deel explicitly encourages. Describe it honestly on applications as an "in-product banner shown to logged-in SMB admins", and submit the public marketing site as the property. Full T&C documents (Deel's Google Doc, the PartnerStack, Impact, CJ and Awin publisher agreements) were not read, so in-app and incentive clauses remain unverified for each program. Never reward a click, for example by unlocking a feature.

## Privacy-safe display networks are closed at this traffic level

The networks that fit OpenHRApp's privacy model will not take it yet. **EthicalAds requires 50,000 pageviews a month**, earns about $2.50 CPM only on EU and North American traffic, and shows house ads elsewhere ([EthicalAds FAQ](https://www.ethicalads.io/publishers/faq/)). It pays through Stripe Connect, PayPal, OpenCollective or GitHub Sponsors ([EthicalAds policy](https://www.ethicalads.io/publisher-policy/)), and none of those is easy from Bangladesh. **Carbon is invite-only**, targets Western developers, pays at low thresholds only via PayPal (wire needs $500 plus a $35 fee), and forbids other networks on the same site ([Carbon FAQ](https://www.carbonads.net/faq); [Carbon placement policy](https://www.carbonads.net/placement-policy)). Ezoic raised its minimum to 250k pages a month in February 2026 ([Ezoic KB](https://support.ezoic.com/kb/article/getting-started-ezoics-requirements)). Media.net pays via Payoneer but wants tier-1 English traffic and uses JS tags ([usro.net](https://blog.usro.net/2024/11/media-net-requirements-for-publishers/)). A-Ads takes any site and pays in BTC ([aads.com](https://aads.com/)). Its ads are mostly crypto and gambling-adjacent, though, and its "plain iframe" embed is still third-party content, so it belongs only on public pages, if anywhere.

EthicalAds and BuySellAds are worth keeping in view because they document **server-side ad APIs**. EthicalAds has an authenticated `POST /api/v1/decision/` endpoint ([EthicalAds API](https://ethical-ad-server.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user-guide/api.html)). BuySellAds has `/ads/{zonekey}.json` with an `embedassets` option that returns base64 images, built for "explicit control over the data exchanged between your users and a 3rd party ad server" ([BuySellAds API](https://www.buysellads.com/docs/ad-serving-api)). A Supabase Edge Function could fetch the creative and the app could render it as a first-party `<a><img>`. Both APIs require forwarding the viewer's IP and user agent and firing impression URLs. Inside a logged-in HR app, that is employee personal data shared with an ad network and needs disclosure. BuySellAds' advertiser attribution pixels can also bring in third-party trackers. Treat this as a later option for a public blog that reaches 50k pageviews, not for the PWA shell. Even at EthicalAds' best rate, 5k pageviews earn about $12.50 a month.

## Direct sponsors and house ads fit a small B2B audience

At low traffic, a sponsor pays for the audience, not the impressions. The audience here is unusually valuable: HR decision-makers at real Bangladeshi companies. Indie tools sell flat monthly slots in the tens to low hundreds of dollars. Fazier sells 30 days for $49 through Gumroad ([Gumroad](https://falaksher.gumroad.com/l/fazier-sponsor/TWENTY?ref=fazier)), and a Launchpedia month sold for $196 ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-got-my-first-full-month-sponsor-and-i-need-your-opinion-fdb484575e)). BuySellAds is out of reach, since it recommends 100k monthly page views and reportedly rejects about 95% of applicants ([ShoutMeLoud](https://www.shoutmeloud.com/buysellads-review.html)). Selling locally has become easier. **Since September 2026, Bangladeshi small businesses can pay for Meta ads with bKash**, against only about 3 million foreign-currency cards in the country ([UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/small-businesses-in-bangladesh-can-now-pay-for-meta-ads-with-bkash/195062)). Local firms already buy digital ads in taka, and a BDT-priced, bKash-payable slot fits how they already pay.

The pitch should be "one exclusive slot seen by HR admins at N companies, with no tracking". The targets are accounting, VAT and payroll consultancies, local POS and accounting SaaS, recruitment portals, coworking spaces, corporate trainers, and bKash/Nagad merchant products. Start at roughly **$20-150 a month or the BDT equivalent**, and let the first one to three sponsors set the real price. A "Sponsor OpenHR" page needs audience stats, slot specs, a price and a payment route: invoice plus bank transfer or bKash merchant for local buyers, and a Payoneer payment request or SWIFT for foreign ones. When the slot is unsold, run a house ad for the paid ad-free plan or swap slots with a complementary indie tool. This costs nothing, adds no risk, and keeps the slot visible to prospective sponsors.

## One redirect pattern satisfies FTC, EDPB and the 2025 Bangladesh ordinance

Every recommended option uses the same mechanism. On the legal side, label the creative itself **"Ad" or "Sponsored"**. The FTC says "Ad:" or "Paid link" is effective and "affiliate link" alone is not ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)). In UK ASA testing, no more than 45% of people recognised "Affiliate" as an ad ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/Insight-recognising-online-affiliate-marketing-same-rules-new-guidance.html)). Mark links `rel="sponsored"`, which can be combined with other values ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links)). A self-hosted image that sets no cookie and reads nothing from the device needs no consent banner. The click counter is the risky part. EDPB Guidelines 2/2023 treat identifiers appended to URLs as "tracking links", with affiliate marketing as the example (para 49). They also say IP collection from user terminals can fall under Art. 5(3) ePrivacy (para 55) ([EDPB](https://edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-10/edpb_guidelines_202302_technical_scope_art_53_eprivacydirective_v2_en_0.pdf)). Bangladesh's **Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 (No. 61)** was gazetted on 6 November 2025. It requires "informed and explicit consent" for personal data, carries penalties of up to BDT 20 million, and its core duties take effect about May 2027 ([Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/18756-personal-data-protection-ordinance-2025-ordinance-no-61-of-2025); [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/bangladeshs-personal-data-protection-ordinance-2025-key-takeaways-4015401)). A counter that holds no personal data stays outside all three regimes.

The implementation follows from that. Each slot is `<a href="/go/{adId}" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer">` around a fixed-size image on OpenHRApp's own storage. The image carries alt text naming the advertiser, a visible "Ad" label, and the destination domain shown underneath. The `/go` handler runs a single aggregate increment keyed by ad ID and day. It returns a 302 to a server-side allowlisted URL, never one taken from a query parameter, which would create an open redirect. It sends `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `Cache-Control: no-store`, sets no cookie, and stores no IP, user ID, tenant ID or user agent. `noreferrer` matters even though browsers default to sending only the origin ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Headers/Referrer-Policy)), because a tenant subdomain would otherwise reveal which customer clicked. Ad links must carry no tenant or user sub-IDs. Ads never appear on attendance check-in/out, camera/selfie, geolocation or payroll-confirmation screens. They appear on dashboard or sidebar screens only, one per screen, with a dismiss button. For paid plans, the server never sends the ad payload, rather than hiding it with CSS. Two cautions apply. Some affiliate terms ban redirects that "cloak" the source; Amazon, for example, forbids obscuring the referring site, though a 302 that lands on the visible merchant URL is generally accepted ([Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/link-cloaking-amazon/)). And sponsor landing pages need a manual review on a regular schedule, so a sponsor's later-compromised page does not drag OpenHRApp's domain down. Even with this design, the destination sees the user's IP once they click. The privacy policy should say so, and should mention the anonymous click count.

Payouts reach Dhaka through **Payoneer, which links to bKash for instant withdrawal** ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/corporate/local/freelancers-now-to-receive-payoneer-payments-through-bkash)), and through wire to a local bank. Wise is unavailable for receiving money. Foreign earnings routed through formal channels as IT/ITES exports can attract Bangladesh Bank's FY2026-27 cash incentive: **2.5% for individual freelancers, 6% for registered software/ITES firms** ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/bb-announces-export-incentives-for-43-sectors-for-fy26-27)). No circular confirms that advertising income qualifies, so check purpose coding with the receiving bank. Local VAT and TDS on sponsorship income also need an accountant's check.

## Conclusion

The ranked recommendation is: (1) Zoho affiliate, the best fit with a wire payout and no traffic gate; (2) a self-sold BDT sponsor slot with a house-ad fallback; (3) Deel and Remote, once the PartnerStack payout to Bangladesh is confirmed; (4) Fiverr and Namecheap via Payoneer as easy-approval fillers; (5) EthicalAds through its server-side API, on public blog pages only, once they reach 50k pageviews; and last, retire the Adsterra Smartlink from the PWA. The usual low-traffic advice says to pick a network that accepts small sites. Here that is backwards. The networks that accept OpenHRApp are the ones that can hurt it, and the money comes from a few trusted names linked by hand.

OpenHRApp's slot works without third-party scripts because it controls the destination as well as the creative. The privacy work is mostly done already: self-hosted image, first-party redirect, aggregate counts. A smartlink undoes it at the last hop by handing the click to an unvetted redirect chain. The next steps are cheap. Send emails to confirm PartnerStack's Bangladesh payout and EthicalAds' terms for logged-in pages, and change a server-side allowlist. The slot's real asset is its audience of HR admins at Bangladeshi companies, and it earns more when sold to the people who want that audience.
