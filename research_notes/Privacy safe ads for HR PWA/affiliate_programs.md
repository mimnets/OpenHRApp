# Affiliate Programs for a Low-Traffic HR SaaS PWA (OpenHRApp, Dhaka operator, no PayPal)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Many official partner pages and help centres (PartnerStack, Fiverr help centre) returned HTTP 403/404 to the fetch tool, so several figures come from search-result snippets or third-party aggregators (getreditus, uppromote, affylist, commissiondex, supademo, Lasso, etc.). Those are marked "(aggregator)" and should be re-checked on the program's own dashboard or terms before relying on them. Primary-source figures are marked "(official)".

## Q1. Commission, cookie, payout threshold/methods, network, traffic requirements, Bangladesh acceptance per program

### Takeaway
The best payout-per-conversion for an HR/SMB audience comes from HR/payroll SaaS on PartnerStack: Deel ($1,500/customer), Rippling ($1,600), Remote (10-15% for 12 months), Gusto ($200+ / recurring). Zoho (in-house, 15% for 12 months, wire transfer) and Fiverr/Hostinger/Namecheap (Awin/CJ/Impact or in-house) are the most practical for a Bangladeshi affiliate without PayPal, because they offer wire or Payoneer payouts. PartnerStack's non-PayPal route is Stripe (Bangladesh not supported by Stripe) or Airwallex direct deposit. Bangladesh on the Airwallex list is unverified.

No traffic minimum or country restriction is stated on the page. — [Deel affiliates](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/)

Paid monthly on Remote's management fees, for 12 months from customer activation. Also listed on Awin (Remote.com FR merchant profile). The official /affiliates URL returned 404 when fetched, so the page may have moved. — [Remote affiliates](https://remote.com/en-us/partners/affiliates); [Awin Remote.com FR](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/75664)

- Impact (QuickBooks, Namecheap, Canva): the minimum is $10 by EFT/ACH/wire or PayPal, and the bank must be in a country Impact supports. - Awin (Fiverr, Remote FR): all international payments go through Payoneer Global Bank Transfer, in local currency or by SWIFT. — [Awin: International Payment Method FAQs](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/International-Payment-Method-FAQs); [Awin + Payoneer](https://www.awin.com/gb/news-and-events/awin-news/faster-international-payments-payoneer)

### Inferences
- Without PayPal, the safest payout routes are Payoneer (via Awin, CJ, or Fiverr direct) and wire transfer to a Bangladeshi bank (Zoho, Hostinger at $500 or more, Impact EFT if Bangladesh is supported). Payoneer to bKash or a bank is the lowest-friction path.
- PartnerStack programs (Deel, Rippling, Monday) pay best per conversion but depend on Bangladesh being supported for Airwallex direct deposit. Stripe does not serve Bangladesh. Verify this before integrating.
- Hostinger's $500 bank-transfer minimum and its 1,000-traffic application rule are hard barriers for a tiny affiliate without PayPal.

### Gaps
- Official current terms for Gusto, Rippling, Remote (page 404), Fiverr (page 403), Canva, Notion, Monday, ClickUp, Grammarly, Semrush and Xero were not read directly. All figures for these are aggregator figures.
- Cloudways, DigitalOcean, Microsoft 365 and Wave: no current terms found.
- Explicit lists of countries accepted at affiliate sign-up (as opposed to payout) were not found for any program, apart from Hostinger's vague "focus regions" clause and Google Workspace's regional eligibility.

## Q2. Do terms allow placement inside a logged-in app / PWA / email? Bans on incentivized clicks, brand bidding, software placement?

### Takeaway
None of the official pages I read explicitly bans or allows a static banner inside a logged-in web app. Explicit bans found are on brand-keyword PPC bidding (Hostinger), self-referral (Zoho) and reuse of brand material (Zoho). Industry codes target downloadable software, toolbars and adware, not a first-party banner in the operator's own web app. Email and newsletters are explicitly encouraged by Deel.

### Cited Findings
- Hostinger bans PPC and pay-per-impression campaigns, bidding on "Hostinger" keywords without approval, display URLs that redirect to Hostinger, and association with adult, political or unlawful content. It says nothing about in-app placement. — [Hostinger Affiliates FAQ](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs)
- Deel lists "socials, newsletters, videos, and more" as channels. The main page does not mention paid search, coupons or incentives; detailed rules are in its T&C Google Doc, which I did not read. — [Deel affiliates](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/)
- Zoho bans self-referral and reproducing Zoho marketing materials, videos or case studies. That could cover reusing Zoho banner creative unless it is supplied via the affiliate dashboard; this point is my inference. — [Zoho Affiliate](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/business.html)
- Industry software codes (IAB UK, Commission Factory) regulate downloadable software, toolbars, rebate pop-ups and widgets carrying affiliate links. Programs commonly ban spyware, adware, parasiteware and cookie stuffing, and may demand brand opt-out from toolbars. — [IAB UK Software Application Code of Conduct](https://iabuk.com/standards-guidelines/software-application-code-conduct); [Commission Factory downloadable software code](https://help.commissionfactory.com/downloadable-software-code-of-conduct); [Wild Apricot affiliate terms](https://www.wildapricot.com/legal-center/affiliate-program)

### Inferences
- A self-hosted image plus a plain link inside the operator's own app is not "downloadable software" in the IAB sense. It is closer to an owned-media placement, like a newsletter. Even so, applications should describe the placement honestly as an "in-product banner shown to logged-in SMB admins and HR users". Some reviewers want a public website URL, so OpenHRApp's public marketing site and blog should be the property submitted.
- Do not reward users for clicking (for example "click to unlock"). Incentivized clicks are a common ban across networks, and per-program wording was not verified.

### Gaps
- I did not read the full T&C documents (Deel's Google Doc, the PartnerStack partner agreement, Impact/CJ/Awin publisher terms, Fiverr's terms), so explicit clauses on "software/app" placement and incentivized traffic remain unverified for each program.

## Q3. Is a tracking script/pixel required, or does a plain tracked link work?

### Takeaway
All the networks found (PartnerStack, Impact, CJ, Awin) and the in-house programs (Zoho, Hostinger) use the merchant's own tracking set on the merchant's site after a click on a redirect or referral link. The affiliate places only a link and, optionally, a banner. No source required an affiliate-side script.

### Cited Findings
- Deel: commissions are attributed through a "unique referral link" tracked in PartnerStack, and banners and logos are available in PartnerStack. — [Deel affiliates](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/)
- Hostinger: "affiliate cookies are stored for 30 days", set after the link click, with last click overwriting. — [Hostinger Affiliates FAQ](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs)
- Zoho: a 90-day tracking period from the first click on the affiliate link. — [Zoho Affiliate](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/business.html)

### Inferences
- A self-hosted copy of the program's approved banner, wrapped in the plain tracking URL, should work for all of these. Network-hosted banner code (Awin/CJ/Impact image tags) loads an impression pixel from the network, so self-hosting the image avoids third-party requests. Some programs may require using only approved creative (Zoho's material-reuse clause), so self-host only creative downloaded from the affiliate dashboard.
- Because tracking is cookie-based on the merchant domain, attribution can be lost in browsers that block third-party or bounce-tracking cookies. Expect some under-counting.

### Gaps
- Not checked: whether any network forbids rehosting its banner images. Network-hosted creatives carry impression tracking; rehosting is common practice, but I found no source either way.

## Q4. Most realistic programs for a tiny Bangladesh-based affiliate to get approved for and earn from

### Takeaway
For approval plus a payout that actually arrives: Zoho (in-house, wire, HR-relevant, strong in South Asia), Fiverr (Payoneer, global, low conversion value but easy), and Namecheap via CJ (Payoneer). For high upside with an HR audience: Deel and Remote (EOR for teams hiring abroad), but only after confirming PartnerStack's Airwallex payout works for Bangladesh. Hostinger is attractive but gated by its traffic rule and $500 bank minimum. Google Workspace referral appears unavailable to Bangladesh residents.

### Cited Findings
- Zoho: no stated traffic minimum, wire payout, $100 minimum, regional data-centre registration (register in the IN data centre for South Asian referrals). — [Zoho Affiliate](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/business.html)
- Fiverr: Payoneer under $1,000 and wire above, $100 minimum; Fiverr is widely used by Bangladeshi freelancers. — [bloggingtips Fiverr](https://www.bloggingtips.com/affiliate-programs/fiverr); [uppromote Bangladesh programs](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/bangladesh/)
- Namecheap via CJ pays Payoneer with a $50 minimum. — [Namecheap payout options](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/9994/2241/payment-options-and-minimum-payout-limits-for-affiliates)
- Deel pays $1,500 per customer and states no traffic minimum. — [Deel affiliates](https://www.deel.com/affiliates/)
- Hostinger requires at least 1,000 traffic and may reject applicants outside its "focus regions". The bank-transfer minimum is $500. — [Hostinger Affiliates FAQ](https://www.hostinger.com/affiliates/faqs)
- Google Workspace referral: Bangladesh not found among eligible regions. — [Google Workspace referral FAQ](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en/landing/partners/referral/faqs/)
- Wise ($75) and Payoneer ($25) refer-a-friend schemes have no audience minimum and are relevant to Bangladeshi SMBs paying or receiving abroad. — [refgrow referral list](https://refgrow.com/blog/referral-programs-to-earn-money)

### Inferences
- Rough fit ranking for OpenHRApp's audience (Bangladeshi and South Asian SMB admins and HR staff):
  1. Zoho People/Books/Workplace: product fit, regional presence, wire payout.
  2. Deel/Remote: a high-value niche for SMBs hiring abroad, gated by the PartnerStack payout check.
  3. Fiverr: easy approval, Payoneer payout.
  4. Namecheap (CJ) and Hostinger: small businesses need domains and email.
  5. Wise/Payoneer referrals.
- US-only payroll tools (Gusto, QuickBooks Payroll, likely Rippling's core market) will rarely convert from a Bangladeshi user base.
- Low traffic means a realistic year-one income of a few one-off commissions. Deel or Rippling's $1,500-1,600 per customer is the only route where a single conversion matters. This is inference, not measured.

### Gaps
- No Bangladeshi forum or blog posts on actual approval or payout experience with PartnerStack, Impact or Zoho were found in this pass.
- I could not verify whether Bangladesh is a permitted affiliate country at sign-up for Impact, PartnerStack or Hostinger.
